## Key Terms

| English | 한국어 | Definition |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Lane | 레인/차선 | Residential road (narrower than a standard road) |
| Grading | 정지작업/그레이딩 | Shaping the ground surface to achieve desired elevations and drainage |
| Swale | 스웨일/배수로 | Shallow channel for directing surface water |
| Crown | 크라운 | Highest point of road cross-section (centre of road) |
| Cross-fall | 횡단경사 | Slope from crown to edge of road |
| Frost depth | 동결심도 | Depth to which ground freezes in winter |
| Vertical curve | 종단곡선 | Smooth transition between two different grades |
| Crest curve | 볼록 종단곡선 | Vertical curve at a high point (hill) |
| Sag curve | 오목 종단곡선 | Vertical curve at a low point (valley) |
| Catch basin | 집수정 | Inlet structure that collects surface water into storm sewer |
| Manhole | 맨홀 | Access structure for underground pipes |
| Invert | 관저 | Bottom inside elevation of a pipe |
| Service connection | 급수/배수 연결관 | Pipe from main to individual property |
| Overland flow | 지표면 유출 | Water flowing on the surface during major storms |
| Freeboard | 여유고 | Safety margin between water level and structure top |
