# Lot Grading and Surface Water Drainage — City of Edmonton

## Core Principle

**Surface water from public infrastructure (lanes, roads) must NOT flow onto private property.**

This is the fundamental rule that governs all grading design in Edmonton.

## Surface Water Flow Direction

- Lane surface water → directed to catch basins or swales within the right-of-way
- Lot surface water → directed away from buildings, toward lane or rear drainage
- No ponding allowed on private property caused by public infrastructure
