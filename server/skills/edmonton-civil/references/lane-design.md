## Edge of Lane — Matching Existing Elevation

**CRITICAL RULE**: The edge of lane must match the existing ground elevation at the property line / right-of-way boundary.

- New lane construction must tie into existing grades at all edges
- No abrupt grade changes at the lane boundary
- Survey existing elevations before design

## Surface Water on Lanes

- Surface water from the lane must NOT flow onto private property
- Lane crown and cross-slope must direct water to drainage infrastructure
- Edge of lane grading must prevent runoff to adjacent lots
