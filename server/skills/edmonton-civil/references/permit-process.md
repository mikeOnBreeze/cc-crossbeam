## Corrections / Review Comments

When the City issues corrections:

1. Receive corrections letter (typically PDF)
2. Address each item with specific reference to standard/guideline
3. Resubmit revised drawings with corrections response letter
4. Mark-up or cloud revised areas on drawings

### Common Correction Categories

- Grading: surface water direction, missing spot elevations
- Alignment: K value too low, grade too steep
- Utilities: insufficient cover, missing connections
- General: missing notes, drawing errors
